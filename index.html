A Little Letter

Lizanne, eighteen months feels like both a heartbeat and a lifetime. I still remember the version of me before you — a little more guarded, a little less soft. You didn't ask me to change; you just made it feel safe to.

Thank you for the ordinary Tuesdays that somehow feel like main character moments just because you're in them. For the mirror selfies and the errands turned into dates, for laughing at things only we find funny, for choosing me on the easy days and the hard ones too.

You are my favorite person to tell good news to first, and my safest place to fall apart in front of. Eighteen months in, and I still get that little flutter when I see your name pop up on my phone. I don't think that's ever going away, and honestly, I hope it doesn't.

Here's to eighteen months of us — and every month after this one, however many it takes to lose count.

Always yours, S'Bonga
